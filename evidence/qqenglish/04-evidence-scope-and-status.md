## Contribution and outcome boundaries / 贡献与成效边界

The applicant guided AI adoption; company staff implemented and operated business systems. The overall organizational outcome is described qualitatively. Departmental reports provide implementation context but do not prove every application was personally built by the applicant. Broad internal value-chain coverage does not establish that all external suppliers, every job or every proposed workflow used a production agent.

本人指导企业 AI 应用，企业员工负责系统实施与运营。整体组织成果按定性表述；部门报告提供应用背景，不证明每个系统由本人独立开发。内部业务链覆盖不等于所有外部供应商、每个岗位或每项建议都已使用生产 Agent。

No savings percentage, measured accuracy, ROI or total headcount is asserted without a reviewed method. Nomination lists and group photographs are not used as deduplicated attendance counts. Meeting durations cannot be summed into the total mentoring hours for the full collaboration.

未在缺少统计方法核验时声称节省比例、准确率、ROI 或累计人数。不把提报名单或合影当去重出席人数，也不把部分会议时长累计成完整合作的指导总时长。
