## Four modules / 四个模块

1. **AI literacy / 认知篇** — Understand model and tool capabilities and their limits in business tasks.
2. **Structured prompting / 提示词篇** — Organize roles, context, tasks, instructions, examples and output formats; use 5W1H to clarify a request and split a complex task into manageable steps.
3. **Workplace scenarios / 场景篇** — Product messaging, marketing copy, creator outreach, short-video scripts, product visuals, sales summaries, operational anomalies and customer feedback.
4. **Department assistants / 智能体篇** — Design role-specific assistants and group exercises around product knowledge, e-commerce operations and brand creativity.
