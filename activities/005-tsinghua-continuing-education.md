## Activity description · English

I designed and delivered AI-assisted training-program design instruction for project managers and instructional designers at Tsinghua University's School of Continuing Education. Drawing on enterprise training practice, I guided the path from business needs to reviewable proposals: strategic, task and individual needs; interviews and surveys; research synthesis; learning plans, course modules and schedules; action learning and enrollment copy. I organized business context, cases, methods and templates as project context and explained the progression from prompts and context to reusable Skills. Resources included 20 prompt templates and two Skill packages for needs assessment and program design, with references and document-generation scripts. I emphasized professional review of AI research and drafts. These methods can inform AI ERP training-project and knowledge-asset workflows and Microsoft Foundry Agent context and tool orchestration.

## 活动说明 · 中文

我为清华大学继续教育学院的项目经理与教学设计人员设计并交付 AI 辅助培训项目设计课程，将自身企业培训实践与学院的项目设计方法结合，指导如何从企业需求形成可审核的培训方案。内容覆盖战略、任务与个人三个层面的需求分析、访谈与问卷设计、调研结果整理、学习方案、课程模块与学时配置、微行动学习和招生文案。我将业务背景、既有案例、方法论与输出模板组织为项目上下文，并讲解从 Prompt、Context 到可复用 Skills 的方法。配套资源包含20个提示词模板，以及需求调研、学习方案设计两个 Skill 包，附参考资料和文档生成脚本，便于教师继续练习与改造。我强调由 AI 辅助研究和初稿生成，关键教学判断与交付审核由专业人员完成。这种需求到方案的流程设计，可为 AI ERP 的培训项目与知识资产管理，以及 Microsoft Foundry Agent 的上下文和工具编排提供参考。
