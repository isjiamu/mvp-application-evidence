## Activity description · English

Since March 2025, I have delivered in-person AI training and ongoing technical guidance for organizations within the State Grid system. I handle needs discussions, course and business-case design, teaching and application guidance for managers, HR staff and business users. I translate prompt engineering, context and tool selection into practical methods. Teaching cases cover substation inspection reports, work plans, energy-policy summaries, meeting minutes, employee training plans and knowledge bases. I explain how to specify context, goals, constraints and output formats, then check results against source material. I also address sensitive information and human review, helping learners develop reusable prompts and approaches to their work. These methods can inform enterprise process improvement relevant to AI ERP, and the design and evaluation of agents using business knowledge in Microsoft Foundry.

## 活动说明 · 中文

自2025年3月起，我面向国家电网体系内相关单位开展多场线下 AI 培训与持续技术指导，结合管理干部、人力资源及业务人员的岗位需求，帮助他们识别 AI 与日常工作的结合点。我负责需求沟通、课程与业务案例设计、现场讲授及应用辅导，将 Prompt 工程、上下文补充和工具选择转化为可操作的方法。课程通过变电站检查报告整理、工作计划完善、能源政策解读、会议纪要、员工培训方案与知识库等案例，讲解如何明确背景、目标、约束和输出格式，并对照原始材料检查结果。我还结合电力企业的工作特点，说明敏感信息处理边界及人工复核的重要性，帮助学员形成可复用的提示词和业务处理思路。这些方法可为 AI ERP 相关的企业流程改进，以及 Microsoft Foundry 中基于业务知识的 Agent 设计与结果评估提供参考。
