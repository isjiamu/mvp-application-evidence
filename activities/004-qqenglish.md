## Activity essay · English

Since 2025, I have worked with QQEnglish as its principal external AI advisor, supporting enterprise AI assessment, training and cross-functional technical mentoring. This ongoing collaboration has lasted for more than a year. My work spans the company's internal value chain: acquisition and sales, trial lessons and follow-up, learner service, teacher quality, content/marketing and product/engineering collaboration, with practical learning tasks for supporting functions. The central outcome is broader AI adoption across everyday work and a more reusable connection between business knowledge, system data, decision criteria and tool steps.

In this collaboration, I provided needs assessment and technical guidance for sales, service, teacher quality management and product/engineering teams. With course consultants, I examined call classification, summaries and scoring, trial-lesson information, follow-up and learning reports, proposing improvements using CRM labels, business rules and human review. For teacher management, I advised on multilingual feedback classification, scoring criteria and AI/human result comparisons. For product/engineering teams, I shared layered knowledge bases, context loading and code-assisted system documentation. I also prepared role-specific Skill examples, mixed-format exercises and a simulated CRM MCP demonstration, turning business requirements into tool-use flows that employees could learn and practise.

Retained departmental materials describe AI-assisted call records, human-reviewed customer replies, classroom analysis, content creation and development work. My contribution is advisory, instructional and methodological; QQEnglish teams implemented and operated their systems. Training in May 2025, cross-functional consultations in May 2026 and a follow-up workshop document milestones across the collaboration. I focused on helping staff connect problems, data, decision criteria and tool steps so they could continue learning, validating and improving. These methods can inform AI ERP sales, service and workforce workflows, and Microsoft Foundry Agent MCP tool use and evaluation.

## 活动说明 · 中文

自 2025 年起，我作为快酷英语主要的外部 AI 顾问，持续参与企业内部 AI 应用的诊断、培训和跨部门技术指导。这段合作已持续一年多，并仍在推进。我的工作围绕企业内部业务链展开，连接获客与销售、试听与客户跟进、学习服务、师资质量、内容营销和产研协作，并通过岗位实操帮助支持部门理解 AI 的应用路径。持续陪跑的核心成果，是 AI 应用逐步进入多个部门的日常工作，使业务知识、系统数据、判断标准与工具步骤能够在团队之间复用。

在具体指导中，我围绕销售、客户服务、师资质量管理与产研协作开展需求诊断和技术辅导。针对课程顾问流程，我与业务人员梳理外呼分类、通话总结与评分标准、试听信息整理、客户跟进和学习汇报，提出结合 CRM 标签、业务规则与人工核验的优化思路。针对师资管理，我指导如何明确多语言反馈的分类规则与评分依据，并设计 AI 与人工结果对照的验证方式；针对产研团队，我分享业务知识库分层、按需加载及由代码辅助整理系统文档的方法。我还准备岗位 Skill 示例、混合格式实操材料和基于模拟 CRM 数据的 MCP 演示，将业务需求转化为可学习的工具调用流程。

现有部门资料记录了 AI 通话信息整理、人工复核的 AI 客服、课堂分析、内容生产及研发辅助等应用。我的主要贡献是顾问、课程与案例设计、判断标准和技术路径指导；企业团队负责具体系统建设与运营。2025 年 5 月培训、2026 年 5 月跨部门咨询和后续工作坊，为持续陪跑提供了跨年度活动依据。我的重点是帮助团队把问题、数据、判断标准与工具步骤连接起来，支持内部人员持续学习、验证和改进。这种跨部门流程与资源编排实践，可为 AI ERP 的销售、服务及人力管理场景，以及 Microsoft Foundry Agent 的 MCP 工具调用与评估提供方法参考。
