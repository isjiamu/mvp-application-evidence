## Activity description · English

I delivered role-based AI training and hands-on guidance for Jindouya/Ziran Cui teams in marketing, online channels, creator partnerships and design. The course drew on 59 pre-training questionnaires and combined teaching, exercises and group co-creation. Examples and templates covered product messaging, marketing copy, partnership outreach, video scripts, product visuals, sales summaries, operational anomalies and customer-feedback analysis. I focused on structured prompts, context and examples, task decomposition, output formats and result checking to help business users embed AI in everyday work. Role-specific templates and reusable steps supported continued practice after the course. This approach to connecting business knowledge, data inputs and AI processing can inform AI ERP marketing, operations and management-analysis workflows, and Microsoft Foundry Agent context and tool design.

## 活动说明 · 中文

我为金豆芽／自然萃团队提供面向岗位任务的 AI 培训与实操辅导，围绕品牌营销、线上渠道运营、达人合作和设计等部门的实际需求，将讲授、练习与分组共创结合起来。课程设计参考59份训前问卷，并把需求整理为可操作的案例与模板：产品卖点提炼、营销文案、达人合作话术、短视频脚本、产品视觉素材，以及销售数据摘要、运营异常分析和客服反馈归因等。我重点指导结构化提示词、背景信息与示例组织、复杂任务拆分、输出格式约束及结果核对，帮助业务人员理解如何把 AI 嵌入日常工作，而非仅体验单个工具。培训材料同时提供岗位模板和可复用步骤，便于团队在课程后继续实践。这种将业务知识、数据输入与 AI 处理步骤连接的方法，可为 AI ERP 的营销、运营和管理分析流程，以及 Microsoft Foundry Agent 的上下文与工具设计提供参考。
